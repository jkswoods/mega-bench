## Creative crafting

- Browse every supported workbench and tier in Creative mode without paying personal unlocks or upgrades.
- Craft without ingredients through Hytale’s native Creative crafting path. Search, categories, filters and batches of 1–16 remain available.
- Switching game modes refreshes the browser automatically. Returning to Survival restores your existing paid unlocks and tiers; Creative access does not grant permanent progression.
- Creative bench controls explain that no payment is needed and cannot consume unlock or upgrade materials.

Native recipe knowledge/world-memory checks, crafting times, output-space checks and server compatibility exclusions still apply. Processing and special plugin stations still need dedicated adapters.

Requires Hytale >=0.7.4 <0.8.0 and Java 25. Back up world/player data before updating. With the game/server stopped, replace the older Mega Bench JAR in UserData/Mods and enable MegaBench in the world’s Mods settings. No custom launcher or separate asset ZIP is needed.

See the [player guide](https://jkswoods.github.io/mega-bench/hytale0.7/) for installation and compatibility. Broader server/mod combinations and actual second-client verification remain unverified.
