# One workshop. Your crafting library.

![Mega Bench](assets/brand/banner.svg)

Mega Bench brings compatible workbenches into one spacious native Hytale interface. Unlock a station with its original recipe ingredients, invest in its next tier and browse its recipes without building a row of separate crafting benches.

Your unlocks belong to you in that world. Replacing the Mega Bench keeps your progress. Unlocking a station pays for its crafting capability; it does not also give you a physical copy of that station.

**Current source candidate:** experimental Alpha **0.1.0-alpha.1**, for **Hytale 0.7.4 / Java 25**. The [CurseForge project](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mega-bench) is created; the final native UI check and first file upload are pending. Start in a backed-up test world and read the [support limits](compatibility.md).

The guide screenshots were captured in development build **0.1.0-spike5r1**. Alpha retains the workshop model and panel layout, with category icons now rendered from loaded native items.

**Experimental preview:** before a more stable 1.0.0, minor updates may change recipes, balance, behaviour or saved data. Back up the complete world and player data before upgrading.

## Make room for a workshop

Craft Mega Bench at a stock Workbench using **8 Iron Ingots, 4 Copper Ingots and 12 wood trunks**. Place it on supported ground, walk up to it and press **F**.

## Three clear panels

[![Current Mega Bench interface showing personal workbench tiers on the left, the recipe grid in the centre and selected recipe details on the right](assets/screenshots/recipe-browser.png)](assets/screenshots/recipe-browser.png)

*Hytale 0.7.4, Mega Bench 0.1.0-spike5r1. Select a workbench to keep its unlock and upgrade controls beneath the source list; the centre remains focused on recipes.*

Search your unlocked stations, switch their original category tabs, compare the ingredients you own and choose a batch of up to 16 crafts. The bottom inventory is a live preview; use the normal inventory screen to move items.

## Bring your other crafting benches

The initial adapters cover ordinary stock crafting benches, **Emberworks Workbench** and **Foundry Floor** when loaded. Emberworks keeps its seven category tabs. Processing and fuel stations, builder benches and special plugin interfaces remain separate stations.
