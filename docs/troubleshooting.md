# Troubleshooting

## The bench or mod does not appear

Check that exactly one Mega Bench JAR is installed, the world has MegaBench enabled and Hytale is version 0.7.4. The current build uses Java 25. A future game update needs a matching mod build.

## An older placed bench asks to be replaced

Back up the test world and player files, then normally break and replace that Mega Bench. The current development build does not automatically migrate earlier tier-one access blocks. Your player capabilities are saved separately.

## My physical station is not unlocked in the library

Owning a physical workbench does not automatically unlock its capability. Select it on the left and pay its original unlock recipe. The purchase does not award another physical station.

## I have the ingredients but cannot craft

Check the selected source's paid tier, recipe knowledge and original prerequisites. Wait for its paid upgrade to finish. Make room for every output in the full chosen batch, finish or cancel the current job, and remain near a placed Mega Bench.

## The station says Adapter required

Use that source station directly. Processing, fuel, builder and special plugin interfaces need dedicated adapters; merely discovering a station does not make its behaviour compatible.

## I cannot move inventory items in the library

The lower inventory is a live preview. Close the library and organise items in Hytale's normal inventory, then reopen it.

## Search shows no recipes

Press **Find** after typing. Check the selected source, category and filter. **All workbenches** searches your unlocked capabilities; select a locked station directly to preview its requirements.

## My progress is missing after switching worlds

Progress belongs to each world and player. Confirm you loaded the expected world with the matching player files. Before a downgrade, restore the complete matching backup rather than combining unrelated player/world/JAR files.

[Report an issue](https://github.com/jkswoods/mega-bench/issues) with versions and clear reproduction steps. Do not post credentials, full player saves or private account information.
