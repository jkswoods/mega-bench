# Your first workshop {#your-first-workshop}

Start with an ordinary stock Workbench and a separate backed-up test world. No fuel, power network or nearby storage is needed for Mega Bench.

Open any screenshot to inspect the full-size image. The photo world uses supplied ingredients for illustration.

## 1. Craft and place Mega Bench {#1-craft-and-place-mega-bench}

At the stock Workbench, combine **8 Iron Ingots, 4 Copper Ingots and 12 wood trunks**. The base recipe takes **5 seconds**; higher Workbench tiers apply the source station's native time adjustment.

Place the bench on supported ground and press **F** while nearby. Opening the library does not automatically import the physical workbenches you already own.

[![Mega Bench placed on a timber workshop platform](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/bench-three-quarter.png)](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/bench-three-quarter.png)

*The actual placed bench, shown in a Creative photography scene. Walk up to its front to open the library.*

## 2. Unlock your first capability {#2-unlock-your-first-capability}

Choose **Workbench** in the left panel. Its original unlock costs **4 wood trunks and 3 rock**. Bring those ingredients, then use **Unlock workbench** beneath the source list.

This pays once for your Workbench capability in this world. It does not produce another physical Workbench. Selecting a locked station previews its recipes and requirements without allowing crafting.

[![Workbench unlock with four wood trunks and three rock available in the left panel](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/workbench-unlock.png)](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/workbench-unlock.png)

*The left panel shows the unlock payment. The right panel independently previews the selected crafting recipe.*

## 3. Browse and craft {#3-browse-and-craft}

Use the original Workbench tabs or search for a recipe. Select its icon in the centre, then check the original inputs, output and required tier on the right. Choose the quantity and use **Craft**.

The live inventory panel is a preview. Close the library to rearrange items in the normal inventory. A full batch needs its ingredients and output space before it can begin.

[![Searching for Bear Trap with the original three Iron Ingot requirement and Craft button ready](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/recipe-ready.png)](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/recipe-ready.png)

*Search by an item name, check the counts on the right, then craft. This example shows three Iron Ingots ready for one Bear Trap.*

[![Completed Bear Trap craft in the inventory preview](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/craft-complete.png)](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/craft-complete.png)

*After the native craft completes, the three ingots have been consumed and the trap appears in the inventory preview.*

## 4. Invest in your next tier {#4-invest-in-your-next-tier}

Select **Workbench**, then **Upgrade** in the left panel. Tier two costs **30 Copper Ingots, 20 Iron Ingots and 20 Linen Scraps**, followed by a **5-second** upgrade. Higher-tier recipes remain locked until the paid upgrade finishes.

[![Workbench tier-two payment showing Copper, Iron and Linen in the left panel](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/workbench-upgrade.png)](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/workbench-upgrade.png)

*The upgrade panel lists all original materials and its five-second duration before you pay.*

The next useful goal is another supported station. Unlock it from the left panel using its original bench recipe and prerequisites. Crafting a physical station as a recipe in the middle does not grant that capability automatically.
