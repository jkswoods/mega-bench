# One workshop. Your crafting library. {#one-workshop-your-crafting-library}

![Mega Bench](/assets/hytale0.7/brand/banner-cinematic.png)

Mega Bench brings compatible workbenches into one spacious native Hytale interface. Unlock a station with its original recipe ingredients, invest in its next tier and browse its recipes without building a row of separate crafting benches.

Your unlocks belong to you in that world. Replacing the Mega Bench keeps your progress. Unlocking a station pays for its crafting capability; it does not also give you a physical copy of that station.

**Hytale 0.7 guide · Java 25 · Development preview.** Choose compatible downloads from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mega-bench) or [GitHub releases](https://github.com/jkswoods/mega-bench/releases), checking the selected file’s exact game-patch requirements and release notes. Start in a backed-up test world and read the [support limits](compatibility.md).

Recipe, stock-craft and compatibility screenshots show the native interface in Hytale 0.7.4. Placed-bench photos and the unlock/upgrade walkthrough are earlier captures with the same model and panel layout.

**Experimental preview:** updates may change recipes, balance, behaviour or saved data. Back up the complete world and player data before upgrading.

## Make room for a workshop {#make-room-for-a-workshop}

Craft Mega Bench at a stock Workbench using **8 Iron Ingots, 4 Copper Ingots and 12 wood trunks**. Place it on supported ground, walk up to it and press **F**.

[Install the mod](installation.md) → [Your first workshop](getting-started.md) → [Unlocks and upgrades](progression.md)

## Three clear panels {#three-clear-panels}

[![Current Mega Bench interface showing personal workbench tiers on the left, the recipe grid in the centre and selected recipe details on the right](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/recipe-browser.png)](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/recipe-browser.png)

*Hytale 0.7.4. Select a workbench to keep its unlock and upgrade controls beneath the source list; the centre remains focused on recipes.*

Search your unlocked stations, switch their original category tabs, compare the ingredients you own and choose a batch of up to 16 crafts. The bottom inventory is a live preview; use the normal inventory screen to move items.

## Bring your other crafting benches {#bring-your-other-crafting-benches}

The initial adapters cover ordinary stock crafting benches, **Emberworks Workbench** and **Foundry Floor** when loaded. Emberworks keeps its seven category tabs. Processing and fuel stations, builder benches and special plugin interfaces remain separate stations.
