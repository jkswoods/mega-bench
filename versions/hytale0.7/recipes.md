# Recipes {#recipes}

Mega Bench reads recipes from the loaded game and supported mods. In Survival, it keeps their original inputs, outputs, duration, knowledge and tier requirements. [Creative crafting](library.md#creative-catalogue) skips ingredient and personal bench payments while preserving native recipe knowledge, memory, time and output checks. Use the in-game detail panel for your installed version; mod updates can change these contracts.

## Make the access bench {#make-the-access-bench}

| Item | Where | Original ingredients | Base time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mega Bench | Stock Workbench | 8 Iron Ingots + 4 Copper Ingots + 12 wood trunks | 5 seconds |

## Unlock supported capabilities {#unlock-supported-capabilities}

| Capability | Original ingredients | Required source | Base time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workbench | 4 wood trunks + 3 rock | Fieldcraft | Instant |
| Emberworks Workbench | 6 Iron Ingots + 4 Copper Ingots | Workbench tier 1 | 3 seconds |
| Foundry Workbench | 8 Iron Ingots + 4 Copper Ingots | Workbench tier 1 | 2 seconds |
| Alchemist's Workbench | 20 rock + 5 Gold Ingots + 10 Venom Sacs + 10 Bone Fragments | Workbench tier 2 | 3 seconds |

These payments unlock a capability; they do not create the physical bench. A physical bench recipe remains a separate craft when its original recipe is available.
